I am a **Research Engineer** at the
[Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics](https://www.x-humanoid.com/en/),
where I explore how embodied intelligence can move from research prototypes
into real-world robotic systems. My work focuses on reinforcement learning and
vision-language-action models for robots that can learn, reason, and act in
complex environments.

Previously, I earned an **MSc in Robotics with Distinction**, jointly awarded by
the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/taught/msc-robotics/)
and the University of the West of England, and conducted research at the
[Bristol Robotics Laboratory](https://www.bristolroboticslab.com/).

## Research Interests

- **Embodied AI** — building intelligent systems that connect perception,
  reasoning, and action in the physical world.
- **Reinforcement Learning** — learning robust robot behaviors through
  interaction, world models, and scalable training.
- **Vision-Language-Action Models** — grounding multimodal foundation models in
  closed-loop robotic control.
