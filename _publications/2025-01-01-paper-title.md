Complex semantic navigation tasks can be decomposed into reusable behaviors. In
this work, we learn two Semantic Navigation Primitives—moving to the middle of
a room and passing through a door—with the DreamerV3 recurrent state-space
world model, then compose them to navigate between rooms in human habitats.

The models are trained with modest commercial compute and deployed entirely on
an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin without cloud access. Individual primitives achieve a
peak success rate of 94%, demonstrating a path toward resource-efficient,
privacy-preserving semantic navigation on embedded robot hardware.
